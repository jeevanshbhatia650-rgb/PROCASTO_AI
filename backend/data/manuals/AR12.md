## 2.1 Operating modes (p.8)
Cool mode holds the room at the target temperature. Fast Cool runs the compressor at full power for 30 minutes. Eco Cool caps the power draw at about 1.4 kW and allows the room to be 1 °C warmer than the target.

## 4.2 Power and energy use (p.19)
The rated cooling draw is 1.8 kW with a 22 °C target and 30 °C outside. Each degree colder on the target raises consumption by about 8 percent, and the compressor draws its peak for 10 to 15 minutes after the target is lowered. Setting 24 °C instead of 18 °C cuts the draw by roughly a third. A clogged air filter raises consumption by up to 15 percent.

## 4.3 Energy saving tips (p.20)
Keep the target between 24 and 26 °C while the room is in use. Close curtains on sunny windows and keep doors shut while cooling. Use the Eco Cool mode overnight.

## 5.1 Cleaning the air filters (p.24)
Clean the two air filters every two weeks. Open the front panel, slide the filters out, vacuum them or wash them in lukewarm water, and let them dry in the shade before refitting.

## E1 Room temperature sensor (p.30)
The indoor unit cannot read the room temperature and falls back to a fixed cycle. Switch the unit off at the breaker for one minute. If E1 returns, the sensor needs service.

## E4 Indoor coil icing (p.30)
Ice formed on the indoor coil, usually because the air filters are blocked or the target is very low. Switch to Fan mode for 30 minutes to melt the ice, then clean the filters.

## CH05 Communication error (p.31)
The indoor and outdoor units cannot talk to each other. Switch the unit off at the breaker for five minutes. If CH05 returns, check the connecting cable or call service.
