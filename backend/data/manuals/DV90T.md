## 1.1 Programs (p.10)
Cotton Cupboard Dry takes about 3 hours for a full 9 kg load. Mixed Load takes about 2 hours 20 minutes. Quick Dry 35 dries up to 1 kg of light items in 35 minutes. The remaining time is an estimate and is corrected by the moisture sensor during the cycle.

## 1.3 Cycle status and finishing (p.12)
When a program ends, the display shows End and the drum tumbles every few minutes for up to 2 hours to prevent creasing. An idle dryer draws about 2 W on standby.

## 4.1 Energy use (p.22)
The heat pump draws 600 to 900 W while drying, about half of a conventional vented dryer. A full Cotton load uses about 1.9 kWh. A clogged lint filter can raise consumption by up to 20 percent and lengthen the cycle.

## E3 Airflow blocked (p.33)
Warm air cannot circulate, so the dryer stops heating to protect itself. On this dryer E3 almost always means the lint filter or the heat exchanger filter is clogged. It is not a water or drain problem.
1. Open the door and pull out the lint filter.
2. Remove the lint by hand and rinse the filter if it feels greasy.
3. Open the lower cover and rinse the heat exchanger filter under running water.
4. Refit both filters and restart the program.

## H1 Heater protection (p.34)
The heat pump got too hot and paused. Make sure the room is below 35 °C and nothing is blocking the air vents at the front. The dryer restarts automatically after it cools down.

## E5 Water tank full (p.34)
The condensed water tank is full. Empty the tank at the top left of the dryer, slide it back until it clicks, and press Start.

## 6.1 Cleaning the lint filter (p.36)
Clean the lint filter after every load. A clean filter shortens drying time and prevents E3. Rinse the heat exchanger filter once a month.
