## 1.1 Cycle guide (p.12)
Cotton runs about 60 minutes at 40 °C and is the everyday cycle for mixed loads. Eco 40-60 runs about 3 hours 20 minutes and uses the least energy. Quick Wash 15 is for lightly soiled loads under 2 kg. The remaining time on the display is recalculated while the load is weighed, so it can jump during the first 10 minutes.

## 1.4 Remaining time and delay end (p.15)
The display always shows the minutes left in the current cycle. Delay End lets the cycle finish at a chosen time, up to 19 hours ahead. If the washer pauses to rebalance the load, the remaining time holds until spinning resumes.

## 4.2 Energy use (p.27)
A Cotton 40 °C cycle uses about 0.9 kWh. Heating the water is about 80 percent of the energy, so washing at 30 °C uses roughly 40 percent less. Peak draw while heating is about 2.0 kW; tumbling and spinning draw 150 to 500 W. Eco 40-60 is the most efficient choice for normally soiled cotton.

## 6.1 Error codes overview (p.40)
The washer shows a code when it stops itself to protect the load or the machine. Codes starting with E point to water supply, drain or balance problems. Note the code before switching the washer off, because the display clears on restart.

## E1 Water supply problem (p.40)
The washer cannot fill with water within 10 minutes. Check that the tap is fully open and the inlet hose is not kinked or frozen.
1. Turn the water tap fully on.
2. Straighten the inlet hose and check it is not frozen.
3. Clean the mesh filter at the hose connection.
4. Restart the cycle.

## E3 Water not draining (p.41)
The washer cannot pump the water out, so it stops with water left in the drum. This is usually a clogged drain filter, a kinked drain hose, or a drain hose pushed too far into the standpipe.
1. Switch the washer off and unplug it.
2. Open the filter cover at the bottom right and put a shallow tray underneath.
3. Pull out the emergency drain tube and let the water run into the tray.
4. Unscrew the drain filter anticlockwise, remove lint and coins, and rinse it.
5. Screw the filter back in firmly, check the drain hose is not kinked, then run Rinse and Spin.

## E4 Unbalanced load (p.42)
The load is too uneven to spin safely, so the washer stops spinning. This happens with a single heavy item such as a bath mat or a pair of jeans.
1. Open the door once the lock releases.
2. Spread the laundry evenly, or add two towels to balance a single heavy item.
3. Restart the Spin cycle.

## DC1 Door not locked (p.42)
The door did not lock at the start of the cycle. Close the door firmly until it clicks and make sure no clothing is caught in the seal, then restart.

## 6.3 Cleaning the drain filter (p.43)
Clean the drain filter once a month, or whenever E3 appears. Always drain the water first with the emergency drain tube, because the drum may still be full. The filter unscrews anticlockwise; never run the washer without it, or water will leak.
